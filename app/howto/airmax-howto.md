# AirMax how-to text (version 1.0.11)

Everything an AirMax cashier can press, and how to do common jobs. Button names are in [square brackets], keys in {curly brackets}.

## Rules of the register
- The top bar has these tabs: CHECKOUT, ORDERS, ITEMS, CUSTOMERS, GIFT CARDS, REPORTS, USERS, SETTINGS, and BACK OFFICE (managers only). Checkout is the sale screen. Esc goes one step back to the sale screen.
- Left of Checkout is the Actions column, right of it are the Transaction Actions, Items, Gift Cards, Lookups and Register groups. Every button shows its key.
- Scan or type a code in the SCANNING INPUT box and press Enter to add an item. Enter on an empty scan box opens Payment.
- A manager is a login with manager rights in AIR POS. Some buttons (for example [Price Change]) need a manager, or ask for a manager approval pop-up.
- The register keeps working when the help service is offline: a sale never depends on Ask AirMax.
Source: Checkout

# How-to topics

## How do I add a customer to a sale?
1. Press [Customer] {F3}, or click the CUSTOMER ADD box under the sale lines. You can scan a card, type a phone number, or search.
2. To find, add, edit or remove a customer, use the window that opens.
3. To take the customer off this sale: press [Clear Customer] {Shift+F3}.
Source: Checkout, Customers

## How do I give a discount?
1. Whole sale: press [Sale Discount] {F6}.
2. One line: select the line and press [Item Discount] {F4}.
3. A promo code: press [Promo Code] {F9}.
4. An extra fee: press [Extra Charge] {F8}.
A discount can need a manager approval. To take tax off the sale press [Tax Off] {F5}; press it again to put tax back.
Source: Checkout

## How do I open the cash drawer?
1. Press [Open Drawer] {Ctrl+D}.
2. The drawer also opens by itself after a cash sale or a cash return.
3. If it does not open, see "The cash drawer does not open".
Source: Checkout

## How do I do end of day, cash in and out, or clock out?
1. End of day: press [End of Day] {Ctrl+E} for the X and Z reports.
2. Cash in or cash out of the drawer: press [Cash In / Out] {Ctrl+K} and choose Pay In or Pay Out.
3. To finish your shift: press [Clock Out] {Ctrl+Shift+X}, count the drawer, then you are logged out.
4. To only sign out: press [Log Out] {Ctrl+Shift+U}.
Source: Checkout

## How do I print a gift receipt?
1. For the sale you are ringing up now: press [Add Gift Receipt] {F10} before you finish. A gift receipt (no prices) prints with this sale. Press it again to remove it.
2. For the last sale that is already finished: press [Gift Receipt] {Ctrl+Alt+P}. It prints straight away.
Source: Checkout

## How do I sell or check a gift card?
1. Sell or reload one card: press [Gift Card] {Ctrl+G}.
2. Sell several cards at the same amount: press [Multiple Gift Cards] {Ctrl+Shift+G}, type the amount once, then scan each card.
3. Check a balance: press [Gift Card Balance] {Ctrl+Shift+B}.
Source: Gift Cards, Checkout

## How do I hold a sale and bring it back?
1. To park the sale you are ringing up: press [Hold] {Ctrl+H}.
2. To bring a held sale back: press [Load Hold] {Ctrl+Shift+H} and pick the held order.
Source: Checkout

## How do I see all the keys?
Press [Keys] {F1}. It shows every shortcut. Every button on the screen also shows its own key.
Source: Checkout

## How do I print a price label?
1. Press [Print Label] {Ctrl+Shift+P}.
2. Scan the items and choose a layout.
3. Print. The sale is not changed.
The label printer is set in Settings, Labels {Ctrl+8}.
Source: Checkout, Labels

## How do I check a price or find an item?
1. Price and stock without adding anything: press [Price Check] {Ctrl+I}.
2. Search the whole catalog: press [Open Finder] {Ctrl+F}, or type in PRODUCT SEARCH {Ctrl+Shift+S}; Enter adds the first match.
3. Find a line already in this sale: press [Find in Sale] {Ctrl+Shift+F}.
4. A sale note that prints under the receipt: press [Sale Note] {Ctrl+N}.
Source: Checkout

## How do I take a payment and finish a sale?
1. Press [Checkout] {F12}, or press Enter on an empty scan box. Payment opens (cash, card, gift card, check, store credit, split).
2. A cash sale is Enter, then {+} to fill the amount, then Enter.
3. For a split payment, type the amount for each line.
4. When nothing is left due, press Enter to finish. One Enter starts the next sale.
Source: Checkout

## How do I change the price of an item?
1. Select the line in the sale.
2. Press [Price Change] (left column). It needs a manager, or a manager approval.
3. Type the new price and confirm.
For an item that is not in the system press [Temporary Item] {F7}, type a price, and press Enter.
Source: Checkout

## How do I change the quantity or remove a line?
1. Click the line in the sale.
2. Press {+} for one more or {-} for one less.
3. For an exact number: type * then the number and press Enter (for example *5). It sets the selected line.
4. To remove the line press {Delete}.
Source: Checkout

## How do I reprint a receipt?
1. Last sale: press [Reprint Receipt] {Ctrl+P}. It prints straight away.
2. An older order: press [History] {Ctrl+L}, find the order, pick it, and press Reprint.
Source: Checkout, Order history

## How do I return an item?
With the receipt (the order is in History):
1. Press [Take Return] {Ctrl+L} and pick the order.
2. Choose the items that are coming back.
3. Press [Checkout] {F12} to finish. A manager may need to approve.
Without a receipt:
1. Press [Return] {Ctrl+R}. The next scans go in as returns (red lines). Press it again to go back to selling.
2. Scan the items, then press [Checkout] {F12} to finish.
Source: Returns, Checkout

## How do I change a setting (printer, sounds, screen size)?
1. Press [Settings] {Ctrl+,}.
2. Printer and drawer: the Printer & Drawer tab {Ctrl+1}. Returns {Ctrl+2}. Screen (scan sound, zoom, sidebars) {Ctrl+3}. Touch {Ctrl+4}. Bulk items {Ctrl+5}. Buttons {Ctrl+6}. Labels {Ctrl+8}.
3. Zoom the whole screen with {Ctrl+=} and {Ctrl+-}. {Ctrl+0} goes back to 100%.
Some settings are for managers only.
Source: Settings

# Troubleshooting

## The cash drawer does not open
1. Press [Open Drawer] {Ctrl+D} again.
2. Press [Hardware check] {Ctrl+Shift+D}.
3. Check the drawer cable to the receipt printer and that the drawer is not locked.
4. Ask a manager if it still does not open.
Source: Troubleshooting

## The register says offline
1. The bottom left shows online or offline. Check that this PC is on the network.
2. Wait a minute and try again.
3. Ask a manager if it stays offline.
Source: Troubleshooting

## The receipt does not print
1. Check the printer is on, has paper, and is plugged in.
2. Press [Hardware check] {Ctrl+Shift+D} to test the printer and the drawer.
3. Open Settings, Printer & Drawer {Ctrl+1} and use the Check printers or Print test buttons there.
4. If it still does not print, ask a manager. Tell them what the test said.
Source: Troubleshooting

## Scanning does not add the item
1. Click the SCANNING INPUT box so the cursor is in it.
2. Scan again, or type the code and press Enter.
3. If it says not found, use [Open Finder] {Ctrl+F} to search by name.
4. If the item has no price, ask a manager.
Source: Troubleshooting

# Limits

## What AirMax and Ask AirMax cannot do
- Ask AirMax explains steps. It can also show where a button is: "Show me" highlights the buttons one by one and never clicks, renames or moves anything.
- "Do it for me" is only for a few safe setting changes (test print, test drawer, hardware re-check, printer choice, drawer type, hardware route, touch keyboard). It always shows a card with the before and after, and nothing changes until a cashier presses Confirm. It can be undone where possible. Every one is written to a local list on this PC.
- It never makes a sale, takes a payment or charge, refunds, voids, returns, changes a price or a discount, edits a customer, manages operators or users, does cash in or out, runs a Z-read or End of Day, or deletes anything. For those, give the steps and tell the person to do them, or to ask a manager.
- When the question is sent, a screenshot of the Checkout screen goes with it so you can see which screen the person is on. Sale lines, totals, the customer box, the Checkout amount and any customer, member, card, order or payment field or popup are painted black. It is view only: you cannot click or move anything from it, and never guess what is behind a black box.
- If the how-to text does not cover the question, reply NO_ANSWER. The app then searches the web with only the typed question (never the screenshot) and shows the result labelled "from the web", or shows a who-to-contact card.
- If the answer is not in this text, say you are not sure and to ask a manager.
Source: Checkout
