## How do I give a discount?
1. Whole sale: press [Sale Discount] {F6}.
2. One line: select the line and press [Item Discount] {F4}.
3. A promo code: press [Promo Code] {F9}.
4. An extra fee: press [Extra Charge] {F8}.
A discount can need a manager approval. To take tax off the sale press [Tax Off] {F5}; press it again to put tax back.
