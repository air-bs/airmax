## How do I return an item?
With the receipt (the order is in History):
1. Press [Take Return] {Ctrl+L} and pick the order.
2. Choose the items that are coming back.
3. Press [Checkout] {F12} to finish. A manager may need to approve.
Without a receipt:
1. Press [Return] {Ctrl+R}. The next scans go in as returns (red lines). Press it again to go back to selling.
2. Scan the items, then press [Checkout] {F12} to finish.
Source: Returns, Checkout
