Air Max 1.0.10
=============
(Air Max was called "Air Register (AIR POS look)" before Oct 30 2026; same app, same settings.)
A separate copy of Air Register with the AIR POS 1.9.11 look, called Air Max. It installs next to the
regular Air Register (own folder, own settings, own updates) but only ONE of the two
can be open at a time: close the regular Air Register before starting this one.

A fast, keyboard-first register for AIR POS stores. It signs in with your normal
AIR POS company, user and password and talks to the AIR POS servers directly.
AIR POS does not need to be installed or open.

Requirements
- Windows 10/11 with Microsoft Edge.
- Internet connection.
- A receipt printer installed in Windows (any 80 mm ESC/POS printer) with the cash
  drawer cable plugged into it. Air Max prints and opens the drawer itself;
  the AIR POS hardware service is NOT needed (if it is installed, Air Max can
  still use it as a backup - see Printer & Drawer settings, Ctrl+,).

What you can do
- Sales, holds, returns (with or without a receipt), reprints, history.
- Payments (full screen): Credit Card first, then Cash, then the rest. Enter moves
  down, + fills the rest, type digits to split, Enter completes when nothing is due.
  Cash sale = Enter, +, Enter. Quick Exact / $5-$100 buttons under Cash. Card terminal,
  keyed card (secure Stripe form - Air Max never sees the card number),
  gift card, check, store credit, split payments.
- Customers (F3): search by name, phone, e-mail or customer number, add, edit,
  attach / remove, see past purchases and return from them.
- Gift cards (right panel): Gift Card (Ctrl+G) sells or reloads one card;
  Multiple Gift Cards (Ctrl+Shift+G): type the amount once, scan each card,
  Enter on an empty box to finish - all paid in one checkout. Balance check too.
- Any receipt printer: Air Max finds the receipt printer by itself, falls
  back to the Windows default, and opens the drawer through that printer.
- Open Drawer button (always on screen, one click) + a drawer log in End of Day.
- Price check, Put Aside / Load Aside, sale notes, calculator, find in sale, zoom.
- End of day: X / Z reports, cash in / out, clock in / out.

Keys (F1 shows the full list in the app)
  Scan           Adds the item           Enter (empty)  Payment
  *5 then Enter  Quantity 5              Delete        Remove line (no popup)
  F2             Quick search            F3            Customer
  F5             Tax Off                 F6 / F4       Order / item discount
  F12 / Ctrl+C   Payment                 Ctrl+Shift+N  New sale
  Ctrl+D         Open drawer             Ctrl+P        Reprint last receipt
  Ctrl+H         Hold sale               Ctrl+Shift+H  Load held sale
  Ctrl+Shift+A   Put Aside               Ctrl+Shift+L  Load Aside
  Ctrl+R         Return (no receipt)     Ctrl+L        History (reprint / return)
  Ctrl+F         Product search          Ctrl+I        Price check (+ stock)
  Ctrl+G         Gift card               Ctrl+Shift+G  Multiple gift cards
  Ctrl+N         Sale note               Ctrl+Shift+F  Find a line in this sale
  Ctrl+Shift+C   Calculator              Ctrl+= / - / 0  Zoom in / out / reset
  F7 / F8 / F9   Unlisted item / fee / promo
  Ctrl+E         End of day              Ctrl+K        Cash in / out
  Ctrl+,         Printer & drawer        Ctrl+Shift+X  Clock out
  Esc            One step back
  Payment: Enter moves down, + fills the rest, digits split, Enter at $0 completes.
  Ctrl+W and Ctrl+Shift+R are blocked so the register can't be closed by accident.

Still done in AIR POS back office: products, prices and stock, staff, store
settings, card-reader setup, and customer points / credit limits.

Settings: Start menu > Air Max > Air Max Settings.
Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AirRegisterAirposLook\logs (no passwords or card numbers are ever logged).
Saved on this PC only: parked (Put Aside) sales, sale notes, the drawer log, zoom.

Updates: Air Max checks for a new version every few hours and installs a
verified update automatically when the register window is closed.

Touch screens: Settings > Touch keypad chooses Air Max keypad, Windows
keyboard, or Off. The on-screen keypad only opens for boxes that need typing;
the scan box never pops up a keyboard. Scanner and physical keyboard work as before.

Register Mode (Settings, manager only): full screen, starts at Windows login,
reopens itself if closed or crashed, and blocks Alt+Tab, Alt+F4 and the
Windows key. Takes effect the next time Air Max starts (use Restart).
Exit to Windows (Ctrl+Shift+Q, or tap the top-left corner 5 times) needs a
manager's AIR POS username and password. Restart / Shut down PC is in the
Touch & full screen settings (a cashier needs a manager login).
Ctrl+Alt+Del cannot be blocked by any app; for a fully locked PC use Windows
Assigned Access / Shell Launcher (needs a Windows admin).
Remote Help (RustDesk) is never closed or hidden by Register Mode.

Remote Help (RustDesk) is NOT part of this app. It is set up and managed from
the regular Air Register; this app never installs or removes it.
When Remote Help is set up on this PC, its ID (ID only, never the password)
shows at the bottom right, next to the version and the clock:
"Remote ID 123 456 789". It also shows in big type on the "Reconnecting to
Air Max..." screen, so it can be read to support while the app is stuck.

Bulk items (Quick Add, right side) can now be grouped in FOLDERS (1.0.5):
Settings (Ctrl+,) > Bulk items (Ctrl+5): Add folder, then search or scan the
products to put in it; Rename, Delete (asks first; its buttons move to the top
level), Move to... and the arrows change the order. Up to 24 spots at the top
(folders + single buttons, single buttons up to 12) and 24 items per folder.
On the sale screen tap a folder to open it, "Back to folders" (Alt+Backspace)
goes back. Alt+1 ... Alt+9, Alt+0, Alt+-, Alt+= press the first 12 spots shown;
the rest are tap-only (the box scrolls). After a sale, a new sale, Hold or Load
Hold the box goes back to the top. Saved on this PC only; AIR POS products are
never changed. Without folders the buttons look and work exactly as before.

A paid sale that AIR POS would not finish (1.0.6):
On the Payment screen "Back to sale (payments kept)" (Esc) closes the screen
only; nothing is cancelled or charged again. A red bar on the sale screen then
says "Paid $X: sale not finished" (and what is still owed) with "Back to
payment (F12)" and "Check AIR POS again", which reads the sale from AIR POS
again and finishes it here if AIR POS already has it done. Each time AIR POS
refuses to finish a sale, the reason is written to the app log.

Customer add box (1.0.7):
Under the sale, three wide boxes: Customer add (F3), Items (Esc) and Checkout
(F12). With no customer the first box says "Scan card, phone or tap to add".
With a customer on the sale it shows the name, the account balance (green, or
red at $0.00 or less), the phone and the points. The balance is the one AIR
POS gave when the customer was added to the sale. The small customer tag above
the cart is still there and still opens the customer. Display only: nothing
about money, payments or AIR POS changed.

Payment pop-up (1.0.8):
Checkout (F12) opens Payment as a pop-up in the middle of the screen; the
sale stays visible but dimmed behind it. Refunds, split payments and "sale
not finished" use the same pop-up. Tapping the dimmed sale does nothing:
press Back (Esc) to go back to the items. Buttons and keys are the same.

Cards on file (1.0.9, Stripe stores only):
Open a customer (Customer, F3) to see "Cards on file". Add card... opens a
form with the name, address and ZIP filled in from the customer; the card is
typed into Stripe's secure card box, and Save stays off until "Customer agrees
to save this card for future purchases" is ticked. Remove asks first. Cards
are kept by AIR POS and Stripe; Air Max never stores card numbers.
At checkout, with that customer on the sale, Manual Credit lists the saved
cards (none is picked for you). Pick one and press Pay to charge it, or type
a new card as before (a typed card is not saved). Cards saved under another
payment channel are shown greyed and cannot be charged. Sola stores and
stores with no card channel do not show cards on file.

Fixes in 1.0.9: Clock In / Clock Out now accept amounts typed like
"1,250.00" or "$100" (they were counted as $0); anything that is not an
amount is refused with a message. An order discount in dollars now keeps
the right tax when items are added or removed after it was given.

Returns without a receipt: Settings > "Refund tax on returns without a
receipt" (on by default).
